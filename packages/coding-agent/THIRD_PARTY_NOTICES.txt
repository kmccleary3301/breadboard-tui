SHA-256: baf02c1e63b7856dbf58368b2a9c7c0a8b2bd4f45d1dee14681418f11d191484

This crate began as a locally-patched copy of brush-builtins from the brush shell
project (https://github.com/reubeno/brush), used under the MIT License, and still
tracks it for the POSIX/bash builtins.

  Copyright (c) 2024 reuben olinsky

Local modifications to the upstream builtins include: implementing
previously-stubbed builtins, the `wait` builtin (PID targeting plus the
`-f`/`-n`/`-p` flags), and cancellation support for the `read` builtin via poll.

It additionally hosts the in-process command-line utility builtins (`src/host.rs`
plus one module per command). Those are ports of third-party programs, previously
carried as separate vendored crates and now consolidated here; the host plumbing
is ours, the utility logic is theirs. Their notices are reproduced in full
below.

Adapted component: the `build_pytest_summary` algorithm from the upstream
`src/cmds/python/pytest_cmd.rs` (pinned at commit
878af7de99e0ba71da2e8fd996f6b52a1836e06c), re-implemented here in
